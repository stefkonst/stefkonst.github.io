B-cube: how the wing is made

The all-DEA wing as built in November 2026. One multilayer sheet is both the skin and the trailing edge: the edge strip is rolled into a cord that also carries the electrode contacts. Discrete and attachment wings are not built yet; their steps will be added here once they are.

The stack

As built
  • Process of Ren, Kim and co-workers (Adv. Mater. 2022): elastomer spin-coated, CNT electrodes transferred through masks, one layer per cycle.
  • A faster spin on the first and top layers makes the ½ D covers, which strengthen the attraction between electrodes once rolled.
  • A four-electrode wing (3 active layers) was also made.

For the next wings

  • Measure the roll length after rolling: it sets the edge tension (Eq. 1).
  • Weigh a cut piece of film for its thickness (Eq. 2), and check it against the capacitance (Eq. 3).
  • Mirror the wing for a second side on the same transmission; keep the outline for the discrete and attachment wings.
  • Electrodes only on the inner half of the roll, with nylon outboard, keep about 90 % of the edge's authority (Eq. 4).

Equations

1. Edge tension from its length

$$\lambda = \frac{L_{wing}}{L_{cut}},\qquad s = 2\left(\lambda-\lambda^{-2}\right)\left[C_1 + 2C_2(I_1-3) + 3C_3(I_1-3)^2\right],\qquad I_1 = \lambda^2 + \frac{2}{\lambda},\qquad T_0 = s\,w\,H_{tot}$$
  • Uniaxial Yeoh, $C_1$ = 42.8 kPa. $\lambda$ = 1.16–1.22 over 60–87 µm of film gives 50–96 mN.

2. Thickness from mass

$$H = \frac{m}{\rho\,A}$$
  • A cut piece of known area $A$ and the elastomer density $\rho$.

3. Capacitance check

$$C = \varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r\,\frac{A_{el}}{H}\,n_{active}$$
  • The formula gives 1.83 nF for the published device, as in the paper; a thinner or thicker layer than assumed shows up here.

4. Part-active roll (series rule)

$$\Delta T = \Delta F\,\frac{k_p}{k_a + k_p}$$
  • An active inner half in series with a stiff nylon outer half keeps about 90 % of the force change at the wing.
