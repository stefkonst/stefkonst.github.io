Active membrane wings

Bat-inspired wings whose membrane is a dielectric elastomer actuator (DEA). The current work is B-cube: a small flapping robot driven by a rolled DEA core, with an all-DEA wing whose skin and rolled trailing edge are both active. The robot is built and flaps; the wing on the bench, the robot's dynamics and models of flight are below.
