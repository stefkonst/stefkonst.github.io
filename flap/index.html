Active membrane wings

Two ways of using dielectric elastomer actuators in a bat-inspired membrane wing. Each page labels its claims as observation, verification or prediction.

Plagiopatagiales

Discrete rolled DEAs embedded in a passive membrane, as in the wings tested so far. Observations from the bench and high-speed video, a verified section model, a 3D design tool in the browser, and the load-path finding: on a free trailing edge the DEAs have almost nothing to pull against.

Full membrane

The whole wing skin is the DEA: two tensioned panels, a batten and rolled edge fibres. A hyperelastic, tension-field model with 3D vortex-lattice aerodynamics sets the tensioning procedure and the shape of the electrode masks. Predictions only; nothing has been built yet.
